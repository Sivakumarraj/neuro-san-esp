The question changed

The hard design question in AI has moved up a level.

Then

How many layers? How do they connect? How big is each one?

Now

How many agents? Who routes to whom? Which model does each one run?

Most teams answer the new question by intuition: add another agent, try a bigger model, ship it.

But the old question already has a well-tested answer.

Idea 1 · 2019
LEAF · Evolutionary Neural AutoML · GECCO 2019

Evolve the architecture, not just the settings.

Cognizant AI Lab's LEAF evolves a neural network's structure: how many layers, how they connect, how big each one is.

The finding that stayed with me

Changing the architecture beat tuning hyperparameters, and the evolved networks were often smaller.

What this does not prove

Three honest limits.

It costs more per answer

The stronger model on the reader made each question cost 71% more, even with fewer tokens.

Structure vs model is not separated yet

The four-agent design was not tested with its own reader upgraded. That is the next experiment.

I'm not the first to search agent designs

My contribution is narrower: a working way to measure and compare Neuro SAN networks, which didn't exist before.
